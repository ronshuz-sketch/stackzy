Terms of Engagement
Effective: March 2026  ·  Version 1.0  ·  How we work together
01
Who Builds Your Product

Stackzy is an engineering studio, not a marketplace, platform, or staffing agency. When you engage Stackzy, you are working directly with engineers who have 2 to 4 years of production experience and come from the Indian Institute of Technology Bhilai, one of India's premier technical institutions.

We do not outsource. We do not hire freelancers on a per-project basis to fulfil client work. Every person who touches your code, architecture, or technical decisions is a permanent member of the Stackzy team who you can speak to directly. This is a deliberate structural choice — we believe that accountability requires continuity, and that the best work comes from engineers who are invested in outcomes, not just deliverables.

You will always know who is working on your project. You will always be able to reach them. There are no intermediaries between you and the people building your product.

02
Starting an Engagement

Every Stackzy engagement follows a clear and transparent onboarding process designed to protect both parties and ensure that work begins on a solid foundation:

  • Initial conversation: A call or exchange to understand your requirements, timeline, budget, and success criteria. No commitment is implied by this conversation.
  • Scope confirmation: Following alignment, Stackzy issues a written scope confirmation outlining deliverables, timeline, team composition, and fees. This document must be approved in writing by both parties.
  • Upfront payment: Receipt of the agreed upfront payment (typically 50%) is required before work commences. This confirms the engagement and reserves engineering capacity.
  • Kickoff: A structured kickoff session sets expectations, confirms communication channels, and establishes the review cadence for the engagement.

Engagements do not begin on the basis of verbal agreements alone. All commitments are confirmed in writing before any work is performed.

03
Communication Standards

Clear, consistent communication is a non-negotiable part of how Stackzy works. Our commitments to you:

  • Responding to client messages within one business day during active engagements
  • Providing written progress updates at least once per week for all active projects
  • Flagging technical blockers, scope ambiguities, or risks immediately in writing — not at the next scheduled call
  • Being available for scheduled calls, reviews, and demonstrations as agreed at kickoff
  • Providing honest assessments of feasibility, timeline impact, and technical trade-offs without softening difficult conclusions

In return, we expect clients to:

  • Designate a single named point of contact with the authority to make product decisions
  • Respond to review requests and questions within the agreed review period (default: 5 business days)
  • Provide complete, accurate, and consolidated requirements rather than iterative corrections across multiple messages
  • Raise concerns or disagreements promptly rather than allowing them to accumulate

Communication failure is one of the most common causes of project delay. Both parties share responsibility for maintaining clear and timely dialogue throughout the engagement.

04
Scope Changes and Change Requests

The agreed scope is the foundation of every Stackzy engagement. We recognise that requirements evolve, and we support reasonable scope changes through a structured process:

  • Any requested change to the agreed scope must be submitted in writing as a formal Change Request
  • Stackzy will provide a written impact assessment within 3 business days, covering the effect on timeline, cost, and technical architecture
  • The change becomes part of the engagement only upon written approval from both parties
  • Work on the changed scope does not begin until any additional fees are confirmed and invoiced

Verbal scope changes are not binding. An engineer agreeing informally to an additional feature in a call does not constitute a scope change. Only written, confirmed change requests are actionable.

Repeated or large scope changes that materially alter the nature of the engagement may, at Stackzy's discretion, require renegotiation of the overall project structure and fees.

05
Quality, Code Standards, and Delivery

Stackzy's quality standards are not negotiable downward. Every deliverable produced by Stackzy adheres to the following baseline:

  • Version control: All code is managed in Git with a meaningful commit history. Commits describe what changed and why, not just what files were modified.
  • Documentation: Every deliverable includes inline documentation and, where appropriate, a README or technical handover document sufficient for a competent engineer to understand, operate, and extend the work.
  • Production grade: Unless a prototype or proof-of-concept is specifically agreed in writing, all code is written to production standards — with proper error handling, logging, input validation, and security considerations.
  • Review process: All critical deliverables undergo internal review by at least one other engineer before being delivered to the client.
  • Testing: Unit tests and integration tests will be provided where agreed in the scope. For blockchain and financial systems, test coverage is considered mandatory regardless of scope specification.

Stackzy provides a 14-day defect correction period following final delivery during which we will fix material defects in the delivered work at no additional charge. This period does not cover new feature requests, changes to requirements, or issues arising from client modifications.

06
Client Responsibilities

A successful engagement is always a collaboration. The Client's responsibilities include:

  • Providing complete, accurate, and timely requirements before and during the engagement
  • Granting necessary system access, credentials, API keys, and repository permissions within 2 business days of the kickoff
  • Reviewing and providing consolidated feedback on deliverables within 5 business days of delivery, unless a different review period is agreed
  • Ensuring that a person with decision-making authority is reachable during the engagement
  • Informing Stackzy promptly of any changes to technical constraints, business context, or requirements that may affect the work
  • Not modifying, deploying, or distributing in-progress deliverables without prior discussion with the Stackzy team

Failures in Client responsibilities that cause delays or additional work will be addressed through the change request process and may affect timelines and fees.

07
Intellectual Property in Engagements

All custom code, designs, and deliverables produced specifically for the Client transfer to the Client upon receipt of full and final payment. Stackzy retains the right to use generic frameworks, reusable utilities, and general engineering patterns from the engagement in future work.

Open-source dependencies included in deliverables remain governed by their respective licences. Stackzy provides a complete list of third-party dependencies and their licences in the technical handover documentation for every engagement.

Unless explicitly prohibited by a separate NDA, Stackzy may reference completed engagements in our portfolio and client list. We will not disclose proprietary technical or business details without explicit written consent.

08
Acceptable Use

Stackzy will not knowingly contribute engineering services to projects that:

  • Violate applicable Indian or international law
  • Facilitate financial fraud, money laundering, or the evasion of sanctions
  • Involve deceptive or manipulative user interfaces designed to exploit users
  • Facilitate creation or distribution of malware, spyware, or other harmful software
  • Involve child exploitation or abuse material in any form
  • Serve as infrastructure for targeted harassment, surveillance, or stalking
  • Otherwise conflict with Stackzy's professional ethical standards, which are applied in our sole discretion

Discovery that an engagement falls into any of the above categories, even after work has commenced, entitles Stackzy to immediate termination with refund of fees proportional to undelivered work and no further liability.

09
Ongoing Retainers and Maintenance

After project delivery, some clients choose to engage Stackzy on an ongoing basis for maintenance, feature development, or technical advisory. These arrangements are governed by a separate written retainer agreement and may be terminated by either party with 14 days' written notice.

Stackzy does not guarantee ongoing availability after a project engagement ends. Clients who anticipate needing ongoing support should raise this during the initial scoping conversation so it can be factored into the engagement plan.

10
Dispute Resolution

Disagreements happen, even in the best working relationships. Stackzy's approach to disputes reflects our belief that most conflicts are resolvable through honest conversation:

  • Direct conversation first: Either party may raise a concern by emailing contact@stackzy.io. Both parties commit to a good faith discussion within 5 business days.
  • 30-day resolution period: If the initial conversation does not resolve the issue, both parties enter a formal 30-day negotiation period. Both sides commit to engaging seriously and transparently during this time.
